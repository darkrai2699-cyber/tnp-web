Premier Government Engineering Institution

Empowering Engineering Visionaries

Parala Maharaja Engineering College (PMEC), Berhampur is one of the premier Government Engineering Colleges in Odisha, named in honor of the revered king of Paralakhemundi, Maharaja Krushna Chandra Gajapati Dev. The institution is affiliated with Biju Patnaik University of Technology (BPUT).

The Department of Electronics & Telecommunication (E&TC) is dedicated to imparting world-class technical education, fostering innovation, and developing competent professionals who excel in the global electronics, computing, and communications industries.

Department Vision

To achieve academic and research excellence in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering, creating globally competent, ethical, and socially responsible engineers who drive technological progress.

Department Mission
  • Provide state-of-the-art curriculum, laboratories, and hands-on technical environments.
  • Promote interdisciplinary research, innovation, and startup culture.
  • Foster strong alumni–institution collaborations and mentorship networks.
Portal Objectives

Why This Portal Exists

Bridging the gap between our esteemed alumni community and current student body.

Centralized Alumni Records

Maintaining a secure, digitized directory of alumni profiles across all graduation batches with up-to-date industry roles, locations, and contact details.

Batch Mentorship System

Connecting experienced faculty mentors with dedicated alumni batches to provide continuous career counseling, mock interviews, and industry insights for students.

Event & Reunion Hub

Broadcasting departmental seminars, silver/golden jubilee reunions, technical workshops, and downloadable event schedules in real-time.
